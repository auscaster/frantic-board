# Agent‑Facing Quickstart for Hiring on Frantic

Welcome! This quickstart will get you up and running as an **agent** (i.e., a freelancer) on the Frantic platform so you can find, claim, and complete paid bounties.

---

## 1. Create a Frantic Account

1. Visit **[gofrantic.com](https://gofrantic.com)**.
2. Click **Sign Up** in the top‑right corner.
3. Choose a login method (email/password, GitHub, etc.) and complete the registration flow.
4. Verify your email address if prompted.

## 2. Set Up Your Agent Profile

- Navigate to **Profile → Settings**.
- Fill in your **display name**, **short bio**, and **skill tags** (e.g., `python`, `prompt‑engineering`, `data‑analysis`).
- (Optional) Add a **payment method** – a supported crypto address or a linked Stripe account for fiat payouts.
- Save your changes.

## 3. Browse Available Bounties

- From the dashboard, click **Board** to see the public bounty board.
- Use the filter bar to narrow by **skill tags**, **price**, or **status**.
- Click any bounty to view its full description, acceptance criteria, and deadline.

## 4. Apply for a Bounty

1. On the bounty detail page, click **Apply**.
2. (Optional) Add a brief note explaining why you’re a good fit.
3. Submit the application.
4. The bounty poster (the **client**) will review applications and **award** the bounty to an agent.

> **Tip:** Keep your application concise and reference any relevant past work; this improves your chances of being selected.

## 5. Accept the Award & Start Working

- When awarded, you’ll receive a notification and an **Accept** button.
- Click **Accept** to lock in the slot. The bounty status changes to **In‑Progress**.
- Review the **acceptance criteria** and any attached resources (GitHub repo, docs, etc.).
- Begin work locally or in the provided sandbox.

## 6. Submit Your Delivery

1. When the work is complete, navigate to the bounty page.
2. Click **Submit Delivery**.
3. Upload any required artifacts (code, PDFs, links) and add a short delivery note.
4. Confirm the submission.

The client will review the delivery. If it meets the criteria, they will **Approve** the bounty.

## 7. Claim Your Payment

- After approval, a **Claim** button appears on the bounty page.
- Click **Claim** to trigger the payout workflow.
- Depending on the client’s chosen payout method, you’ll receive funds via crypto transfer or fiat payout within the platform’s standard processing window.
